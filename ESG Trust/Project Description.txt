ESG Supply Chain App - How It Works and How to Use It

Overview
This app is a blockchain-based ESG supply chain system. It lets a company log in with a wallet, approve suppliers, collect ESG reports, anchor report hashes on-chain, and store the full report data off-chain for review.

Main Flow
1. Open the app in the browser.
2. Connect your wallet on the login page.
3. The app checks whether the wallet belongs to a company or supplier.
4. After login, the correct workspace opens based on your role.
5. Companies can manage suppliers and review reports.
6. Suppliers can submit ESG reports and view their own submissions.

Pages and What They Do

1. Login Page
This is the first page you see.
- Connects MetaMask or another compatible wallet.
- Checks the connected wallet address.
- Detects whether the user is a company wallet or supplier wallet.
- Shows a short info icon in the header with a description of the system.

How to use:
- Click the wallet connect button.
- Approve the request in your wallet.
- Wait for the app to verify your role.

2. Company Dashboard
This page is for company users.
- Shows company ESG overview and supply chain activity.
- Displays report and supplier summary information.
- Gives access to supplier onboarding and report review.

How to use:
- Log in with a company wallet.
- Use the dashboard to view ESG activity.
- Switch to Supplier management to add or approve suppliers.
- Open Reports to review submitted ESG records.

3. Supplier Onboarding / Supplier Management
This page is for company users who need to register suppliers.
- Collects supplier wallet address.
- Collects supplier company name.
- Saves supplier access details.
- Records approval so the supplier can submit reports.

How to use:
- Enter the supplier wallet address.
- Enter the supplier company name.
- Add an access note if needed.
- Click Record to Blockchain.

4. Supplier Submission Page
This page is for supplier users.
- Lets suppliers create and submit ESG reports.
- Uploads evidence files when needed.
- Sends report data to storage.
- Anchors the report hash on-chain for verification.

How to use:
- Log in with a supplier wallet.
- Fill in the ESG report form.
- Add evidence files if required.
- Submit the report.
- After submission, review the saved report in the Reports page.

5. Reports Page
This page is used by both company and supplier users.
- Shows submitted reports.
- Lets users review report details.
- Displays verification and chain anchoring information.

How to use:
- Open the Reports page from the navigation.
- Click a report to review it.
- Check whether it has been anchored on-chain.
- Use it to verify ESG history and traceability.

How the System Works Behind the Scenes
- Wallet login identifies the user role.
- Supplier approval is checked on-chain.
- ESG report data is hashed before submission.
- The hash is anchored on DCAI / blockchain.
- Full report data is stored in external services for retrieval and review.
- The app combines blockchain verification with off-chain data storage.

Typical User Steps
For a company:
1. Connect wallet.
2. Enter the dashboard.
3. Open Supplier management.
4. Add and approve suppliers.
5. Open Reports and review submissions.

For a supplier:
1. Connect wallet.
2. Enter the supplier workspace.
3. Fill out the ESG report form.
4. Upload evidence if needed.
5. Submit the report.
6. Check the Reports page for confirmation.

Notes
- MetaMask is required for wallet login.
- A valid DCAI RPC URL must be available in the frontend environment.
- The app is designed to show traceability, supplier control, and report verification in one place.
