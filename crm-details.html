CRM — Walkthrough

Screenshots below show the CRM interfaces: admin dashboards, sales rep views, lead and interaction history, calendar follow-ups, and report generators.

Technical Highlights

  • Dual-role dashboards: Role-based access control separates admin and sales rep views; permissions enforced server-side.
  • Lead lifecycle: Tracks leads from creation through conversion; integrates interaction history and follow-up calendar.
  • Calendar integration: Scheduled follow-ups sync with a calendar view; reminders and notifications keep reps engaged.
  • Report generation: PDF/CSV export with configurable date ranges and metrics; admin and rep-level reporting.
  • Interaction logging: Every contact, call, email, or note is timestamped and searchable for audit trails.
  • Inventory management: Product catalog and stock tracking tied to lead and customer records.
