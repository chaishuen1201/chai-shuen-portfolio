ESG Trust — Walkthrough

Key screens and demo for the ESG Trust supply chain app. The demo video shows anchoring and report flows; screenshots show report generation and chatbot UI.

Technical Highlights

  • Blockchain integration: MetaMask wallet login; smart contracts manage supplier approval and role detection.
  • On-chain anchoring: Report hash is anchored to a blockchain (DCAI) for immutable proof of submission and timestamp.
  • Off-chain storage: Full report data stored in external services for privacy and scalability; only hash anchored on-chain.
  • Dual workspaces: Company users manage suppliers and review reports; supplier users submit ESG evidence and track submissions.
  • Evidence management: File uploads with validation; stored separately from blockchain for easy retrieval and audit.
  • Supply chain traceability: Companies can verify supplier credentials and report history; creates transparent ESG accountability.
